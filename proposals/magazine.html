From the editor
— Stephen Hilton
Napa, 2026

Why a family archive, and why now?

We started this site as a favor to our future selves. At some point, everyone forgets the names of the neighbor's dog, the exact wording of the joke, the month we took that trip. An archive is an act of kindness to the people you're going to become.

There's nothing prestigious on these pages. Our dog is ordinary. Our opinions are mostly wrong. We're trying anyway. If the archive is a little beautiful, that's a happy side-effect.

Somewhere between the blog posts and the pet pictures, you'll find everything important: a household that takes its people seriously and itself not at all. Welcome in.
