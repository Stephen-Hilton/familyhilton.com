Full Text

At MakeInfinite Labs as Head of GTM, I owned all enterprise accounts directly, and helped other SEs and sales with all accounts. One challenge was staying on top of the many communication channels: Telegram, Signal, Slack, Discord, WhatsApp, text messages, and email. Email was least used—except for enterprise customers.

While closing a deal with a $6B enterprise, I missed a customer email. It wasn’t critical, but it delayed closing by about a week, hurting responsiveness. This highlighted we were juggling 20+ prospects and moving too fast. Dropping small start-ups was acceptable; dropping enterprise customers was not.

To fix this, I blocked 30 minutes twice weekly for “enterprise follow-up.” I reviewed last communications with each enterprise account and ensured forward momentum. I also increased interaction standards, automating smaller customer paths and limiting them to 30-minute intro calls until they signed up.

This simple discipline prevented further misses, ensured responsiveness for strategic accounts, and balanced GTM focus across large and small customers.
