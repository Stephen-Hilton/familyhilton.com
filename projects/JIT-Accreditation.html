Full Text

One of the first investors and close partners of MakeInfinite Labs was a Web3 company called Chainlink Labs, a private company that by Sept. 2025 exceeded $100B in total value secured (TVS), and one of the largest and most respected names in the Web3/blockchain space.

As part of our tight partnership, we often engaged in joint opportunities. In mid-2024, we were asked to co-host their largest hackathon event of the year. As Head of Solutions, it was my responsibility to ensure our SE, Marketing, and Engineering teams were ready for success.

Two weeks before the event, our partner informed us that the event would no longer be a hackathon, but a training/accreditation event. The problem: MakeInfinite Labs had no training content or accreditation program, and there was no time to source and implement a new vendor solution.

Brainstorming with Marketing led to three options: back out (damaging reputation), show up with minimal activities and no formal program, or improvise a verifiable program using existing systems. I chose the third. Having written all product documentation and telemetry reporting, I tested and proved a method to verify user actions through backend telemetry monitoring.

With approval, I cleared my calendar and built a 3-phase program (Users, Analysts, Developers). Within one week, I published all training materials and deployed telemetry processes. The last update went live as SEs were flying in.

The event was a success: over 200 User accreditations, ~50 Analysts, and ~10 Developers completed. We generated 28 prospects, several becoming customers, and strengthened goodwill with a critical investor/partner. The program endured post-event, remaining a popular onboarding path for new users.
