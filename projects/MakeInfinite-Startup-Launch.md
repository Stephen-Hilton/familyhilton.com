# Overview

### Situation:
- Recruited by two founders (CEO, CTO) to join new AI/ZK tech startup
- First role as Executive Chief of Staff with 4-word mandate: go start the business”
- Startup lacked internal systems, compliance framework, and GTM readiness

### Tactics:
- Probing questions to identify leadership pain points
    - What is your biggest pain-point right now?
    - What keeps you up at night?
    - Where are you running out of time?
- CTO overloaded, risk of missing alpha release deadline
- CEO concerned about product adoption and market objections
- Three-part plan: 
    - proxy decision-maker for CTO
    - own internal systems
    - address sales objections
- Prioritized CTO time and focused CEO concerns on adoption

### Actions:
- Scaling the CTO:
    - Acted as L1 proxy decision-maker for engineering, escalating only urgent unknowns
    - Largely took over as the technical expert during VC fundraising  
    - Freed CTO for higher-level decisions and product delivery
- Scaling the Organization and Operations
    - Set up Jira, CMS, customized Notion-CRM integrated with pipeline
    - Managed SOC2 compliance vendor selection and program initiation
- Prepared for GTM
    - Conducted competitive pricing research and positioned pricing strategy
    - Collaborated with Marketing on release positioning and buzz generation
    - Initiated Customer Zero: used platform internally, logged 400+ Jira tickets
    - Built Jira dashboards to gamify ticket creation and encourage adoption

### Results:
- CTO delivered alpha product on time (Dec 2022)
- Closed first customer by Dec 2022, three more in first-half of Jan 2023
- Achieved $4K ARR in first 30 days, $3M ARR in year one, $14M ARR in year two
- Fastest startup to $1M ARR (until ChatGPT surpassed)
- Customer Zero surfaced 400+ issues early, improving customer experience
- Earned trust of founders, shaped early GTM and product processes
- So much so, next mandate was two-word "start GTM" as Head of GTM

# Full Text
I was still working at Teradata as their Global Director of Strategy, Analytics and Automation, when two past colleagues reached out, looking to see if I would join their newly funded startup, MakeInfinite Labs. Both founders were known to me—the CEO an SVP at a VC, the CTO a VP of Cloud engineering—talented leaders and friends, so it was an easy “Yes.”  

When I joined, we were still starting the business, so they asked me to serve as Executive Chief of Staff and "start the business.” That was my entire job description. Without other direction, I began asking open-ended questions:  
- What is your biggest pain-point right now?  
- What keeps you up at night?  
- Where are you running out of time?  

This revealed the CTO was stretched too thin, risking alpha release delays. The CEO worried about adoption in a mature market. I developed a three-part plan: (a) act as proxy decision-maker for engineering, (b) own internal systems setup, and (c) address sales objections.  

Over six months, I acted as L1 decision-maker, escalated only urgent issues, and freed the CTO to focus on higher-level needs. I set up Jira, organized content in a new CMS, migrated CRM from Excel to customized Notion-CRM, and integrated pipelines.  As we started gearing up for a Strategic fundraise, I often replaced the CTO on calls with VCs, to answer questions and provide technical details, again helping the CTO stay heads-down.

I also prepared for customer objections: selected SOC2 compliance vendor, built pricing research and strategy, collaborated with Marketing on messaging, and initiated Customer Zero, using our own platform internally. I personally logged over 400 Jira tickets and gamified reporting with dashboards.  

These efforts enabled the CTO to deliver the alpha on time (Dec 2022). That same month, I closed our first customer, followed by three more in Jan 2023. Within 30 days, we reached $4K ARR; within a year, $3M ARR; by year two, $14M ARR. We briefly held the record as fastest startup to $1M ARR until ChatGPT.  

As an epilogue, once we started bringing on customers in Jan. 2023, I was given a new role, Head of Solutions / GTM, and given a new four-word directive: "build go-to-market", and was directly responsible for driving that $14M ARR, along with a small 5 person team.

The Customer Zero program was a major success, surfacing 400+ issues over 2 years (2/week) before customers experienced them, directly improving product quality.
