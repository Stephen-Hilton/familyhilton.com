Customer Zero: Dogfooding for Product Quality

Launched MakeInfinite Labs’ Customer Zero program, running the company on its own SXT technology. Engaged all departments, built telemetry pipelines, and exposed pain points early. Opened 400+ tickets over 2 years, resolving issues before customers saw them, drastically improving customer experience and product maturity.

Overview

Situation:

  • MakeInfinite Labs initiative: “Customer Zero” (dogfooding) program
  • Objective: Run SXT internally on its own SXT technology
  • build into operational processes (HR, onboarding/offboarding, employee access)
  • Goal: uncover issues before external customers experienced them

Tactics:

  • Product immaturity caused significant internal pain
  • Many at company didn't have reason to use our own product

Actions:

  • Coordinated across HR, engineering, sales, and operations for rollout

  • Established onboarding/offboarding workflows through SXT platform

  • I forced GTM:

    • Built internal data pipelines for telemetry + CRM integration
    • Unified customer master: product usage + CRM interactions in one schema/report
    • Acknowledged the discomfort, but long-term customer satisfaction worth it

  • Implemented telemetry pipelines for internal product usage, to lure Engineering

  • Logged and tracked issues encountered internally

  • Partnered with engineering teams to prioritize fixes
  • Advocated for internal experiences as proxy for external customers
  • Shared learnings broadly to improve adoption readiness

Results:

  • 400+ product issues fixed before reaching customers
  • Gamified with Jira reports (and Eng. resolution reports)
  • Improved product maturity and reliability
  • Company-wide alignment with product realities
  • Customer satisfaction improved by experiencing rough-spots early

Customer Obsession:

Proactive quality focus, protecting external customer experience.

  • Ownership – Initiated and led Customer Zero program, involving entire company.
  • Customer Obsession – Eliminated 400+ points of friction before reaching customers.
  • Dive Deep – Experienced product end-to-end, across HR, telemetry, CRM.
  • Earn Trust – Delivered mature product experience, avoiding customer frustration.
  • Insist on the Highest Standards – Forced product to meet real-world usage before release.
  • Deliver Results – Improved product maturity, reduced churn risk, enhanced adoption.

One of the programs I started at MakeInfinite Labs was “Customer Zero,” sometimes known as “dog-fooding,” aka running the SXT company on SXT technology. This involved interacting with organizations we normally didn’t work with, such as HR to set up onboarding and offboarding processes for employee access. This forced the entire organization to dive deep into using our own technology as a customer. Because of the immaturity of the product, this was a massive pain. However, we experienced the pain first, before customers, which is 100% preferable. In the 2 years of the program, we opened over 400 product tickets to fix issues that we, as customer zero, had encountered before our customers. I see this as massively successful; that’s 400 points of frustration removed from the product so the prospect never saw them – the customer experience was exponentially improved because of this program.
