Career — a working timeline

Twenty-odd years of turning messy data into shipping products — with a couple of life markers in there too. Full résumé: stephen.skillsnap.me.

  • 2026 — presentSkillSnap.me — Solo founder
    AI résumé & cover-letter SaaS. Concept → revenue-positive in <8 weeks. Serverless AWS (Bedrock, Lambda, DynamoDB, SQS, S3, CloudFront, Cognito); a multi-model LLM + RAG generation pipeline; Stripe billing, pricing, automated support.
  • 2025 — presentPaydaay (stealth fintech) — Fractional CTO / CPO
    AWS serverless architecture; a Rust + Postgres data platform; AI-agentic, auto-healing infra; five-9s transaction systems. Runs the college intern program.
  • ~2024Two kids became two teenagers
    One deep in theater & K-pop, one deep in ecology & biology — both deep in D&D. The annual trip continues: Scotland, France, Greece.
  • 2022 — presentMakeInfinite Labs — Chief of Staff → Head of Solutions/GTM → Strategic Advisor
    Grew a pre-launch AI product from $0 → $3M ARR (12 mo) → $14M ARR (24 mo). 900+ technical stakeholders, 20+ conference talks, the first Rust CLI and the Python SDK, the public docs.
  • 2010 — 2022Teradata — Sr. Solution Engineer → Director, Western US → Global Director, Strategy / Analytics / Automation
    $80M+ quota, ~50% of US compute capacity (Apple, Netflix, Meta, LinkedIn, eBay, PayPal, EA). Later: a telemetry & analytics framework across 2,000+ accounts / $2B ARR with board-level reporting. Petabyte-scale Hadoop / Spark / Kafka platforms in the early years.
  • 2010sPut down roots in Napa County, California
    Two adults, two kids, a golden retriever named Sunny — and a standing plan to go somewhere, usually Europe, at least once a year.
  • 2009 — 2010Lunexa Consulting — Sr. Technical Consultant
    Enterprise Data Warehouse for Visa; KPI trees for Yahoo!; data engineering for Wells Fargo & RealNetworks.
  • 2001 — 2009Best Buy Corp. — Data Analyst → Manager → Sr. Manager, Infrastructure & Innovation Center
    Led ~30 BI engineers serving 4,000+ stores; retail-innovation pilots delivered ~18% labor-efficiency gains.
